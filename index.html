Framework

How We Read the Industry

Materials

Producers and brands — shingles, underlayment, decking, fasteners, and drainage. We track price action, raw input costs, and manufacturer pricing announcements.

Distributors

The specialized wholesale supply chain connecting manufacturers to contractors — warehouses, fleet, and credit facilities serving regional installers.

Contractors

Businesses specializing in installation, repair, maintenance, and waterproofing — the primary service provider layer for residential and commercial work.

Specialty Tech

Drones, AI measurement, next-gen solar shingles, smart materials, and the CRM/estimating/field platforms reshaping how the industry runs.
